The Journey

Day by Day

Sept 11 · D1

Arrive in Jaisalmer

Fly BLR → JDH, drive to Sam Sand Dunes. Check in at Le Royal Camps after 3 PM. Evening desert settle-in, welcome drinks, bonfire & dinner at camp.

🏕️ Le Royal Camps Dinner included
Sept 12 · D2

Explore Jaisalmer

Full day — Jaisalmer Fort, Patwon Ki Haveli, Gadisar Lake in the morning. Afternoon camel ride (complimentary at camp 4:30–6 PM). Sunset at Sam Dunes. Evening folk music, cultural performance & DJ at camp.

🏕️ Le Royal Camps BF + Dinner incl. 🐪 Free camel ride
Sept 13 · D3

Jaisalmer → Jodhpur by Road

Check out by 10:30 AM. Drive ~285 km in the Urbania Tempo Traveller. Lunch stop at Khusbhoo Haveli Restaurant, Pokhran. Arrive Jodhpur, check in at The Ummed after 2 PM.

🚐 ~5 hrs drive 🏨 The Ummed Jodhpur
Sept 14 · D4

Jodhpur Heritage Day

Mehrangarh Fort (morning, 3–4 hrs), Jaswant Thada, Toorji Ka Jhalra stepwell. Afternoon at Ummed pool. Evening Clock Tower market walk & Mishrilal Lassi.

🏨 The Ummed Jodhpur BF + Happy Hrs
Sept 15 · D5

Jodhpur Immersion

Blue City heritage walk, Umaid Bhawan Palace museum, Rao Jodha Desert Rock Park. Shopping at Sardar Market. Dinner at Mehran Terrace (fort-view restaurant).

🏨 The Ummed Jodhpur 🍽️ Mehran Terrace dinner
Sept 16 · D6

Departure

Check out by 12 PM. Last-minute shopping if time permits. 8 AM airport drop in Tempo Traveller. Fly JDH → BLR.

Good to Know

Travel Tips

September is late monsoon — warm, possible showers, and fewer crowds.

☀️ Weather

  • 28–38°C, warm and humid
  • Possible monsoon showers — carry a light rain jacket
  • Desert evenings can be pleasant
  • Shoulder season — fewer tourists, better prices

🎒 Packing

  • Light breathable cotton clothes
  • Comfortable walking shoes (forts have steep paths)
  • Sunscreen SPF 50+, sunglasses, hat
  • Light scarf for temples & sand
  • Power bank, camera, reusable water bottle
  • Carry Aadhaar / Passport for hotel check-in

💡 Pro Tips

  • Visit forts early morning to beat the heat
  • Carry cash — many shops & vendors are cash-only
  • Negotiate at markets — start at 40–50% of asking price
  • Stay hydrated — desert heat is deceptively draining
  • Mishrilal Lassi near Clock Tower is non-negotiable
  • Rooftop cafés in both cities = best views
  • Mehran Terrace at Mehrangarh — book ahead for the 15th
